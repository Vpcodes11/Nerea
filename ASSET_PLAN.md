# NERÉA assets

Original fictional brand, copy, label and intersecting-leaf emblem. The visual reference is Organimo's connected ocean journey: https://organimo.com/ . NERÉA uses its own identity and product geometry.

## Connected 3D world

Four reading compositions share one camera route: night crag and clouds, a coastal wrack garden over muted dawn water, the exploded capsule study inside a stone canyon, and a tidal ribbon sculpture rising above the water. Chapters are 125, 125, 125 and 100 viewport heights. The opening uses the supplied reference's actual mountain mesh and texture atlases, with the NERÉA jar placed among its peaks. Warm product light, a cool rim light and wider environment light panels shape the jar. Desktop opening perspective follows the reference's focal-length/film-gauge relationship of 100/100, giving a vertical field of view of about 31.418° at 1280 × 720, then blends to 42° for the subsequent chapters; the phone retains a 42° lens. Desktop pointer movement shifts the camera by up to ±0.25 units horizontally and ±0.11 vertically; the phone response is smaller. The coastal garden's restrained marine palette and left-aligned editorial copy replace the former pink tree, glass sphere, shell, stepping stones and floating outcrops.

SOURCE.json records original transforms, camera, texture selection and material behavior. NERÉA rotates the geometry by π around Y and applies a uniform scale of 1.9 for the direction and proportions of its camera route. This reproduces the rock shape and surface with adjusted framing, without claiming that the whole page is pixel-perfect.

Implementation: src/reference-mountain-material.js. The NERÉA jar retains its independently authored material and lighting.

Ownership remains with the reference site; no CC0 claim is made for this asset. The independently rebuilt cloud system uses 372 layered spatial wisps: 110 tilted foreground wisps around the opening jar, 96 low distant wisps and 166 elevated wisps arranged in seven asymmetric banks at different distances. Varying horizontal aspect ratios and individual low-cloud height fades break up the straight shoulder. Elevated banks have no sea-level height cutoff, allowing clouds to occupy the upper corners and sky behind the product. The system uses instanced textured planes, not volumetric ray marching. It includes blue-grey directional shading, soft scene-depth intersections, slow drift and rotation, water reflections, an underwater fade and stable reduced-motion poses. A separate alpha-tested depth draw preserves the coastal portrait's actual leaf silhouette when clouds pass behind it.

## Rendering behavior

Water is hidden in the opening and begins fading in once travel progress exceeds 0.45, avoiding a water-plane cut through the reference cliff. Calm water caches its reflection between updates at up to 30 Hz, with refreshes during camera travel and pointer-driven view changes. A separate depth layer includes solid cloud occluders and excludes transparent light rays, bubbles and points. The animation-frame loop sleeps when hidden, inactive or holding a reduced-motion composition, then wakes for relevant changes. Capsule and finale animation updates run only when those objects are visible. Shared geometry, instanced clouds and coral, spatial culling, smaller phone rock meshes in later chapters and cached desktop shadows remain in use.

The earlier generic-boulder revision recorded 26 draw calls and 229,482 triangles on reflection-reuse frames, and 36 draw calls and 341,170 triangles on reflection-refresh frames. Those historical counts do not describe the current opening, which uses different geometry and hides its water plane. See QA.md for current validation; performance observations are conditional to the checked composition and device.
